**Founder’s Office — Sales & Partnerships Assignment**

---

## 1. Why Malaysia & Target Customer Profile

* **Regulatory Catalyst:** Malaysia's nationwide **LHDN MyInvois e-invoicing mandate** has forced thousands of SMEs to digitize how they *report* invoices, exposing a massive operational gap in how they actually *collect* and *reconcile* payments.
* **Target Customers:** Malaysian B2B SMEs in **wholesale distribution, light manufacturing, and commercial services** (RM 3M – RM 50M annual revenue).
* **Core Pain Points Today:**
  * Finance teams waste hours chasing overdue payments manually over WhatsApp and email.
  * Incoming DuitNow, GIRO, and FPX transfers arrive without structured invoice references, creating month-end reconciliation bottlenecks.
  * Extended Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) of 40–60+ days chokes SME working capital.

---

## 2. Why YYC Global Business Solutions (#1 Partner Pick)

* **Cloud Leadership:** Malaysia's pioneering **Xero Platinum Champion Partner** (since 2017), with Managing Director Janet PJ Lim sitting on the Xero Partner Advisory Council Malaysia.
* **Distribution Scale:** Multi-hub footprint across Kuala Lumpur, Penang, and Johor, plus high-engagement SME tax summits (500+ business owners per event).
* **Mutual Win:** Volopay AR eliminates manual bank-statement cleanup for YYC's bookkeepers while giving YYC a recurring revenue share and a sticky value-add for their Xero SME client base.

## 3. Interactive Prototype Walkthrough (`index.html`)

The live browser prototype demonstrates the complete end-to-end Volopay AR workflow across three interactive views:

1. **SME Dashboard (`1. SME Dashboard`):** Create and send LHDN-validated e-invoices with embedded payment links and monitor real-time auto-reconciliation metrics.
2. **Customer Payment Portal (`2. Customer Pay Link`):** Experience the buyer's frictionless checkout flow supporting **DuitNow Instant**, **FPX Corporate Banking**, and **Corporate Cards**.
3. **Xero Auto-Sync Ledger (`3. Xero Auto-Sync`):** Verify how paid invoices are automatically matched to incoming bank references and pushed straight into Xero with zero manual entry.
