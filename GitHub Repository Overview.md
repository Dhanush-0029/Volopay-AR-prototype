# Volopay AR — Malaysia Partner Expansion & Interactive Prototype

**Target Market:** Malaysia (SME Wholesale, Distribution & Light Manufacturing)  
**Anchor Partner (#1 Pick):** YYC Global Business Solutions (Xero Platinum Champion Partner)  

---

## Live Interactive Prototype
This repository hosts a zero-setup, browser-based interactive demo of **Volopay Accounts Receivable (AR)** built for Malaysian SMEs and cloud accounting partners:
1. **Create & Send Smart Invoice:** Generates an LHDN MyInvois-validated invoice with embedded local payment rails (DuitNow, FPX B2B, Corporate Card).
2. **Buyer Checkout Simulation:** Demonstrates one-click settlement from the buyer's perspective.
3. **Zero-Touch Auto-Match & Xero Sync:** Automatically reconciles incoming funds to the exact invoice and pushes a clean ledger entry into Xero.

## How to Launch on GitHub Pages
1. Upload `index.html` and `README.md` to your public GitHub repository.
2. Navigate to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` and folder `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
4. Your live prototype will be accessible at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.
